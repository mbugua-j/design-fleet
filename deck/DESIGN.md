# The deck - design notes

The deck describes all thirteen Design Fleet stations. The harbour illustration
shows a clearly labelled nine-station delivery subset, not the complete roster.
The station files and `crews/roles.json` are authoritative for model defaults.

## Structure and evidence

The twelve slides cover the fleet, task separation, roster, delivery subset,
Pipeline, backward handoffs, cost measurement, asset production, delivery,
shared UI contract, dashboard and installation.

`dashboard-demo.png` illustrates the real dashboard with synthetic account,
project, run and consumption data. It is not performance evidence. No private
product screenshot or unverified throughput measurement is included.

`index.html` contains the editable Pipeline cards and SVG connectors;
`pipeline.css` styles them, and `pipeline.js` updates connector positions.
The verifier compares the diagram's stations, labels and routes with app sources.

BuildR combines an agreed PR set, not every open PR. Watchkeeper discovers fresh
scope and records durable receipts. The production-ready route includes Draftsman
even though the historical nine-station delivery illustration does not.

## Presentation

The deck uses a monochrome palette, grayscale artwork, editorial spacing and
light/dark themes. Categories remain distinguishable through labels, icons,
rail thickness and surfaces rather than color alone. Inactive slides are inert;
links do not advance the deck, and reduced-motion behavior is supported.

The deck is authored in HTML; it does not claim parity with a separate Figma
file. Artwork was generated by the project creator. Font and Fluent icon notices
are retained in this directory.
